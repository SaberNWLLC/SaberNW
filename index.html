Our Mission

Safety isn't a requirement.
It's how the work gets done.

The demolition and industrial industry has a fundamental problem: safety is still treated as a checkbox rather than a core part of how work gets planned, sequenced, and executed. Contractors react. They don't build it in.

Saber NW exists to close that gap — not by handing over binders full of procedures nobody reads, but by engaging every level of a project, from ownership and executive leadership down to the supervisors and workers who do the actual work.

Built-in safety is worth more than a thousand administrative controls. And the demo contractor succeeding is everyone succeeding.

What We Do

Built-In Safety.
Not Bolted On.

We provide high-level safety and project leadership for demolition, abatement, environmental, and complex industrial work — as an owner's representative, client advocate, or embedded field safety consultant. Wherever the work is, we go.

01
Owner & Client Representation

You need someone in your corner who actually understands what's happening on that site — not just what the contractor reports. We serve as your eyes, voice, and protection on demolition and high-risk projects, keeping your interests front and center from pre-construction through closeout.

02
Construction & Demolition Safety Oversight

Field-present safety leadership for demo and heavy construction projects. We know the machinery, the sequencing, the hazards, and the people doing the work. Real oversight from someone who's been on both sides of the fence.

03
Demolition & Abatement Planning Support

Pre-demolition hazard reviews, site-specific safety planning, work planning support, and sequencing oversight for complex structural, industrial, and environmental remediation scopes. We understand what's feasible and what isn't — because we've done it.

04
Site Safety Audits & Field Inspections

Unannounced or scheduled field audits that go beyond checklists. We assess real conditions, real behavior, and real risk — then communicate findings in plain language to both field crews and executive leadership.

05
Safety Program Development

Programs built around how work actually happens — not templates that gather dust. We develop practical safety systems that field crews can understand, supervisors can enforce, and owners can trust. SOPs, JHAs, site plans, and training frameworks.

06
Incident Investigation & Corrective Action

When something goes wrong, you need real answers — not a report written to minimize liability. We conduct thorough root-cause investigations that find what actually happened, protect your organization, and prevent recurrence.

07
Regulatory Compliance — OSHA & L&I/WAC

Federal OSHA and Washington State L&I/WAC compliance support including citation response, EMR improvement strategy, audit preparation, and client-facing safety narratives. Compliance is the floor — we help you build above it.

08
Project Risk Management

Risk register development, pre-project hazard identification, and ongoing risk monitoring for high-exposure scopes — asbestos, lead, silica, PCBs, confined spaces, crane and critical lifts, fall hazards, and heavy equipment operations.

09
Training & Supervisor Coaching

Practical training that field supervisors can actually use. We develop and deliver toolbox talks, supervisor leadership training, and hands-on coaching that builds the safety culture from the middle out — where it actually has to live.

10
High-Risk Work Planning

Specialized planning support for confined space entry, crane and rigging operations, critical lifts, torch cutting, structural removal, bridge and infrastructure work, and high-reach demolition. Plans that reflect how the work will actually be executed.

About David Saber

Started in
the Field.
Never Left It.

Saber NW was built on a simple premise: the best consultant is the one who's been where your workers are standing.

David Saber didn't start his career in an office writing safety programs. He started as a laborer at Envirocon in 2010, learning demolition, abatement, and environmental remediation from the ground up. He progressed into equipment operation, took on foreman-level responsibility, and moved into the Health and Safety Department around 2015 as a Safety Technician — bringing his field knowledge directly into the safety role.

That foundation has defined everything since. Working across industrial, commercial, demolition, abatement, and heavy civil environments, David advanced through safety leadership roles including Regional Health & Safety Manager and ultimately Director of Health & Safety — overseeing company safety programs, audits, incident investigations, claims management, regulatory compliance, and supervisor development at scale.

Saber NW is the next chapter: bringing that full-spectrum experience directly to owners, clients, and project teams who need real oversight, not a clipboard consultant who's never touched the work.

Saber NW is a Washington State Women and Minority-Owned business, built to deliver elite safety and project leadership wherever the work demands it.
